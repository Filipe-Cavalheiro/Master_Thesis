
@olfati-saberConsensusCooperationNetworked2007 Distributed Formation Control: Multivehicle systems are an important category of networked systems due to their commercial and military applications. There are two broad approaches to distributed formation control: i) representation of formations as rigid structures  and the use of gradient-based controls obtained from their structural potentials and ii) representation of formations using the vectors of relative positions of neighbouring vehicles and the use of consensus-based controllers with input bias. We discuss the later approach here.
