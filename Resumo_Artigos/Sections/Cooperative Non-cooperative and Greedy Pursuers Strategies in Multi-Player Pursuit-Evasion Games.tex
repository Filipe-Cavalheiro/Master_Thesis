There were defined 3 types of games:
\begin{enumerate}
\item 1 target to many chasers but the chasers communicate with each other.
\item 1 target to many chasers but the pursuers are competing among themselves in their effort to capture the target
\item 1 target to many chasers but each pursuer decides to ignore all other pursuers and pursue the evader on its own 
\end{enumerate}

The results show that in 1. the best results where reached 2. was second and in 3. the capture of the target was not possible.
