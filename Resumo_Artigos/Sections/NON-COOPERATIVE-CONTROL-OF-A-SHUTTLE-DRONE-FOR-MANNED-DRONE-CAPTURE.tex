Rotating wing drone controller: Non-linear Model Predictive Control (NMPC).\\
Differential games: min max is used to decide the action of each drone

The UAV model can be written as:

\[
\dot{\mathbf{p}}_a = \mathbf{V}_a
\]

\[
\dot{\mathbf{\Phi}}_a = E(\mathbf{\Phi}_a)^{-1} \boldsymbol{\omega}_{B_a}
\]

\[
\dot{\mathbf{V}}_a = m^{-1} \mathbf{R}_{IB}(\mathbf{\Phi}_a) \left( \mathbf{f}_e + \mathbf{f}_a \right) - g \mathbf{R}_{IB}(\mathbf{\Phi}_a)^T \mathbf{z}_i
\]

\[
\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_{B_a} = I^{-1} \left( - \boldsymbol{\omega}_{B_a} \times (I \boldsymbol{\omega}_{B_a}) + \boldsymbol{\tau}_e + \boldsymbol{\tau}_a \right)
\]

Attitude control: the controller used in this thesis is the one from the paper: \cite{7535979}\\

Therefore, the new RUAV model state and input vectors can be defined as
\[
x_a = \begin{bmatrix}
P_a & \Phi_a & V_a & \omega_{Ba}
\end{bmatrix}^T,
\quad
u_a = \begin{bmatrix}
f_{ez} & \tau_{ex} & \tau_{ey} & \tau_{ez}
\end{bmatrix}^T .
\]

Non-Linear internal controller: Mellinger controller

Simplified RUAV model: The previous drone model is complex this one is simpler


\begin{align*}
    \dot{P}_a &= V_a \\
    \dot{\psi}_a &= \omega_{a_z}
\end{align*}
