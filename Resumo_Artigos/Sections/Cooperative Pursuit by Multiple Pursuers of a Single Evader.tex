In this paper Operational research is used in order to have multiple drones to one. IN A 2D STATE SPACE!!!

Interesting facts: this research started with the chase of s ship with two other ships in 1925: [1] (of the paper)

Apollonius Circle: we can draw circles made form the chaser and the target, on the intersection of 2 circles (considering 2 chasers and 1 target)
its a point where both chasers can reach the target. This is dominated the (BSR) or border of safe region, btw safe region here dominates the safe
region for the target, meaning inside the Safe Region (SR) the target can not be captures. The idea is to enclose the target inside 
a SR in order for it not to be capable to escape and then to reduce the size of the SR until the drone is captured. The target 
wants to stay equally distance of all the BSR edges in order to keep it self safe from capture.
